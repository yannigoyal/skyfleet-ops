<objective>
Finish TEST-04 by covering the three scenarios that exercise the console's harder-to-observe
surfaces: the visualizations, the AI flight director running against its deterministic mock, and
the telemetry stream's ability to recover from a network interruption on its own.

Purpose: these are the scenarios where a test is most easily written to pass without proving
anything. A chart assertion can count SVG nodes that exist whether or not the data reached them;
a reconnect assertion can reload the page and observe a connection that was never actually
recovered. Each spec here is written so that removing the behavior it names makes it fail.
Output: the last three spec files, a full suite covering all six TEST-04 scenarios plus the
health check, and a `tests/README.md` that describes what exists instead of what was planned.
</objective>

<planner_assumptions>
**Flagged assumption — the SSE disconnect mechanism carries residual risk.** 04-RESEARCH.md logs
this as assumption A2 at medium risk: Playwright's request routing intercepts new requests
rather than tearing down an already-established streaming connection, so the reliable trigger is
installing the route abort and *then* reloading, which forces a fresh EventSource that fails
immediately. That reload is legitimate because it happens before the disconnect assertion. The
reconnect half must not reload — see this plan's prohibition. If the abort proves unable to flip
the indicator in practice, `context.setOffline(true)` is the documented fallback; it is noisier
because it also breaks the two unrelated 5-second polls, and choosing it must be recorded in the
SUMMARY rather than swapped in silently.

**Flagged assumption — heatmap cell labels are asserted permissively.** `FleetHeatmap` hides a
cell's text when the cell is narrower than 40px or shorter than 28px. With ten near-equal cells
in a 640x280 treemap every cell is far above that, but an active mission from an earlier spec
enlarges one cell and shrinks the rest. The spec therefore asserts that *at least one* cell
carries its drone id and battery percentage as SVG text, rather than all ten — enough to prove
the non-colour cue exists without coupling the assertion to treemap layout arithmetic.
</planner_assumptions>
