<objective>
Give the operator per-drone depth: a battery sparkline in every roster row, and a detail
panel that opens on click showing the selected drone's battery, altitude, and speed over
time alongside its current mission.

Purpose: the roster today shows a single instantaneous number per field. A drone at 62%
battery and falling fast and a drone at 62% and stable look identical, which is exactly the
distinction a dispatcher needs. The sparkline restores the trend at a glance and the detail
panel restores it in depth.
Output: a widened telemetry hook, the sparkline column, and the detail panel mounted in the
console grid — with the same selected-drone state the heatmap will reuse in plan 05.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| SSE telemetry stream → client memory | An unbounded server-driven event stream accumulates into client-side arrays |
| SSE telemetry values → DOM | Server-supplied numerics render directly as readouts and chart coordinates |
| Selected-drone identity → detail panel render | The selection can point at a drone that has since left the fleet |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-14 | Denial of Service | Unbounded telemetry history growth in `useTelemetryStream` | medium | mitigate | All three series share one push-then-shift cap at `maxHistoryPoints` (default 120, roughly one minute at the 500ms SSE cadence), implementing D-03. An acceptance criterion asserts exactly three cap comparisons exist — one per series — so a new series cannot be added without its bound. |
| T-03-15 | Tampering | Telemetry numerics rendered as detail-panel readouts | low | mitigate | Format every readout through `.toFixed(n)` following the `FleetRosterPanel` precedent, so a non-finite value cannot render as raw `NaN`. Chart series are numeric arrays consumed by Recharts, never interpolated into markup. |
| T-03-16 | Repudiation | Stale telemetry shown for a drone that has left the fleet | medium | mitigate | The detail panel checks snapshot membership before rendering any readout and shows the explicit "Drone offline or removed from roster" message otherwise, so last-known values are never presented as current. Pinned by the held-out backstop test. |
| T-03-17 | Information Disclosure | Drone identifiers rendered in chart labels and readouts | low | accept | Drone ids are non-sensitive fleet labels already displayed throughout the console and returned by unauthenticated endpoints; no additional exposure. |
</threat_model>
