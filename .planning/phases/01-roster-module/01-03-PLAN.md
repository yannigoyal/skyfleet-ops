<objective>
Close out the roster module by proving every failure mode the operator can hit returns a correct,
documented response instead of undefined behaviour, and by locking the ROST-04 layering contract
with an executable assertion.

Purpose: the tracer (plan 01) and the removal slice (plan 02) delivered the happy paths. This is the
validation-and-error-states slice from the tracer-first pattern. Its user-facing delta is that a
dispatcher who mistypes a drone id, double-adds a drone, or submits an empty field gets a specific
status code and reason rather than a 500 or a silent no-op — and that the module's layering cannot
silently regress in a later phase.

Output: `test_models.py`, `test_repository.py`, an extended `test_router.py` error matrix, and a
behavioural assertion that the router's write handlers go through `roster.service`.
</objective>
