`POST /api/roster` and immediately see it in `GET /api/roster` with live telemetry merged in, served
by the real FastAPI app.

`main.py` construction-timing problem (RESEARCH Pitfall 3) after one commit instead of after the
whole module is built on top of it.

`__init__`), the `main.py` wiring change, and the `backend/tests/roster/` test package with
end-to-end coverage of the add-and-list path.
